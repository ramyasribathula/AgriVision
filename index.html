AI Crop Prediction

This feature should only produce predictions after training and evaluating a model with historical, crop-specific farm data.

Recommended model inputs
  • Farm boundary and crop area
  • Crop type, variety, and sowing date
  • NDVI and NDVI trend over time
  • Temperature, humidity, rainfall, and wind
  • Soil moisture and soil-test data
  • Pest, disease, and farmer field observations
  • Historical yield and irrigation records

Do not publish crop-yield or disease predictions until the model has been evaluated with independent real farm data and reviewed by agriculture experts.
